# 🌳 1 Trillion Trees

Interactive climate simulation created with React as part of a bachelor thesis.

##  About the Project

This project was developed as part of a bachelor thesis to explore how interactive climate simulations can help users better understand abstract climate goals and environmental challenges.

The website allows users to interact with climate related systems and simulations in an intuitive way. The goal is to make difficult and often abstract sustainability targets more understandable and emotionally tangible through interactivity and visualization.

The project focuses on the question:

> Does an interactive, simulation-based climate model improve users
understanding of the long term impacts and limitations of reforesta-
tion, emission reduction strategies and related factual climate knowl-
edge?

## 🧠 Research Purpose

Many climate goals and environmental statistics are difficult for people to fully understand because they are abstract and disconnected from everyday experience.

This project investigates whether interactive digital experiences can:

- increase engagement,
- improve comprehension,
- and create a stronger emotional connection to climate topics.

The simulation was designed to make complex climate-related processes more accessible and understandable for users.

## 📖 Usage

When opening the website, users are guided through the functionality with an integrated tutorial that explains how to use and interact with the simulation.

The tutorial introduces the controls, interactions, and the climate related concepts presented within the project.
